Season guide

From camp to core

Before Week 1

Set the roster

Matchmaking combines alliance groups of up to 200 survivors into four camps. The two strongest alliances in each state enter roster lock first. Organise membership before the lock.

Weeks 1–9

Open the routes

Start at a Level 1 Camp Outpost if your camp has no territory. East and West open first, followed by North and South. Secure connecting Outposts and Passes to reach the centre.

From Week 3

Merchant Guild

The client lists the first battle on Thursday at 12:00 server time, lasting one hour and repeating every seven days. Check your event screen for the live window.

Week 10

Fight for the Ark Core

Mining Site and Pass ownership locks. The Ark Blockade Zone offers priority access toward the Forbidden Zone where the final Core contest takes place.

Mining Sites, Passes and crystal production

R4s and leaders share a declaration attempt pool between Mines and Passes. Mining Site declarations begin with a shared 60-second window. Once battle starts, allies in the same camp contribute to one capture meter. The first camp to reach 100% wins, and the guide estimates about 30 minutes of control for a full Mining Site capture. Ownership can change once per day. Stage troops before the fight, then reinforce when the contest opens.

Mining Sites and Passes produce Aberrant Crystals. Monday 00:00 server time is the weekly settlement point in the client. Newly captured Level 2 and 3 Mines boost the first two outputs. Exact yields and boosts remain unresolved.

Outposts, movement and Sunday invasions

Outposts connect territory and have their own challenge attempts. Neutral Outposts award Merits for fighting guards and damaging durability. Once held, durability damage earns Merits. Where several alliances contest an owned Outpost, the leading Merit total wins when durability reaches zero.

The controlling camp can travel through a Pass. Declaring on a Pass can also open a teleport staging area for eligible members. Sunday invasion windows can open enemy-camp targets beyond normal borders. Check the live map for eligibility and invasion effects before moving.

Dino Scales, buildings and Relic Skills

Dino Scales upgrade the Antiviral Spore. Client references cover output, storage and a level cap upgrade. The Dino Hunting Turret is named in the guide. Its combat numbers and upgrade costs remain unknown. Relic Skills return with changes, and invasion text points to possible higher troop losses during applicable periods.

Dinosaur Boss, Rescue and other findings

The client contains Dinosaur Boss targeting, troop rotation and durability logic, plus Rescue map objects and chat sharing. The encounter rules, schedules and rewards await live confirmation. The guide found four internal minigame layouts and separate Genesis Lab DNA records. The Genesis Lab link to Season 8 remains speculative.
