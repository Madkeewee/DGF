Black Gold Battlefield

Enter each player’s strongest march CP using the same in-game screen. The top four float, and the strongest two are primary and backup alloy carriers. The other 16 are split across four defence squads with comparable CP.

How to run the plan

Confirm your starting side and update the objective names before sharing. Hold the Factory perimeter and Military Base, use the Hospital squad for sustain, and have the Refineries squad hold nearby points. Floaters can teleport to reinforce when called.

Stage the floaters at the Rare Earth Factory a few minutes before each alloy spawn. Check the live event timer. Once the primary or backup carrier picks up the alloy, the other floaters escort them to HQ. Defence squads hold their positions unless the caller redirects them.
